Privacy

Last updated: [date]. Draft for Richie to check before launch.

Junbo Food (ABN [ABN]) respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect on junbofood.com and how we use it, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles and the Spam Act 2003.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join our list.
  • Your vote for our next product, if you choose one.

We don't use cookies or advertising trackers. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device's IP address when the page loads (see policies.google.com/privacy).

Why we collect it

To tell you about new batches, launches and our online shop, and to decide what to make next. We only email you because you asked us to.

Who can see it

Your details are stored with our email provider, Kit (kit.com), which stores data in the United States. We never sell or share your details with anyone else.

Unsubscribing and your details

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing [hello@junbofood.com].

Questions or complaints

Email us first and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
